Executive Summary & Program Overview

By synthesizing automated gap analyses (leveraging NIST CSF 2.0 evaluation frameworks), third-party vendor risk scoring, policy exception tracking with compensatory controls, and agentic AI workflow automations, this dashboard provides clear, actionable intelligence for both technical and executive stakeholders to maintain risk at an acceptable level as the business scales.

Key Risk Findings & Treatment Tracking

1. Supply Chain Risk Management Gap (GV.SC-01)

Domain: Third-Party Risk Management | Severity: High

Third-party logistics partners connecting into regional marketplace endpoints lacked automated vulnerability reporting and continuous attestation workflows.

2. Legacy S3 Data Archival Policy Exception

Domain: Policy Exceptions & Data Protection | Severity: High

Engineering requested an exception to standard AES-256 storage encryption requirements for historical analytical data dumps due to migration complexity.

3. AI Compliance Agent Permission Boundary Drift

Domain: AI-Related Risk Governance | Severity: Medium

Internal risk automation AI agents possessed overly broad write privileges over risk register database tables without supervisory approval checkpoints.

4. Quarterly Access Recertification Latency

Domain: Identity & Access Management | Severity: Low

Manager reviews for internal administrative access privileges experienced periodic scheduling delays, mitigated via automated reminder scripts.
